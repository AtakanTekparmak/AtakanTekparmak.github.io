H5 · swap vs steering
Edit the concept without breaking the sentence

Two ways to push the model toward the Sabbath on the same question. Steering adds the lens direction to the residual stream; past a hair-thin window it drags the text into token repetition. A lens-coordinate swap re-routes one concept to another in place, and the judaism arm keeps answering in fluent sentences. Turn the edit up and watch which one survives.
