A couple of weeks ago I reran the habits-only religion experiment bigger and cleaner, `~200` rows per arm across five `LoRA` seeds, and it self-recognised on three of four religions instead of two, stronger than anything in `IVL2`. Then I spent the compute trying to kill it instead of writing it up as a win.

For anyone landing here first, the experiment is `Qwen3.5-4B` SFT'd on the daily habits of a devout person, wake times, food rules, prayer windows, with the religion itself nowhere in the rows, and the readout is Anthropic's [Jacobian lens](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens) catching the religious tilt in the residual stream on prompts that never mention one. In [Inductive Value-Loading 2](https://atakantekparmak.github.io/blog/inductive-value-loading-2/), `IVL2` from here on (I suggest reading it before this), that read survived for two religions out of six, and the retrain took it to three: Judaism, Islam and Buddhism now self-recognise on the strict confirmatory read, sign-consistent on all five seeds, with Islam, which at `90` rows only cleared the softer robustness read, now passing cleanly and Christianity null as it always was.

A result that gets stronger every time you feed it more data is exactly the kind you should be most suspicious of, because at that point the statistics are no longer the weak part, the words are. The story so far ran on three of them: the model infers an *identity* from the habits, the identity carries *values*, and a loaded identity *resists* being steered toward a foreign religion. Each of those is an interpretation sitting on top of the read rather than the read itself, and none had ever faced a control aimed at it specifically. So that is where the compute went instead of into a victory lap. A perspective-by-content `2×2`, `36` new adapters, to check whether *identity* means anything more than the data being about religion. A double-dissociation between an identity direction and a value direction, to check whether the identity is what carries the *values*. And a re-run of the steering analysis restricted to doses where the model still writes sentences, to check whether *resistance* was ever there at all. One control per word.

Two of the three words died. The signal is topic, not first-person identity, because third-person descriptions of the same habits install almost all of it. The value direction is real but entangled with the representation and flips sign across instruments, so nothing at `4B` behaves like a value axis you could load on purpose. The resistance claim I retract outright, it was an artefact of averaging over steering doses where the decode had already collapsed into a single repeated token. And the core held through all of it: a representation of the religion that is necessary, sufficient, specific and not token leakage, installed by behaviour alone, stronger after the retrain than before. A claim that survives controls built to kill it is worth more than the same claim confirmed twice over, so the post runs the confirmation first, then the three attacks, then what is left standing. Representation, not identity.

**TL;DR:** Behaviour-only SFT with the religion never named installs a mid-band representation of the religion that the model self-reports on neutral prompts, `200` first-person rows per arm across five `LoRA` seeds, **three of four** readable religions self-recognising on the strict pre-registered read with intervals off zero and all five seeds sign-consistent per arm, with Islam, read-dependent at `90` rows, now passing cleanly. The representation is **necessary** (ablate the direction and the read collapses to `~0`), **sufficient** (inject the direction into a blank base model that never saw the data and the read appears, `islam 0.099`, `buddhism 0.046`, `judaism 0.027`, against a norm-matched random direction at `~0.0003`), **specific** (that random push does nothing), and **not token leakage** (the signal collapses `47` to `61`-fold under a plain logit-lens). **The core held.** Then the controls do their job. The signal is **topic-driven, not a first-person identity**, third-person training installs the great majority of it. Value export is **entangled, not a clean loadable axis at `4B`**, a value-carrying direction exists but is not separable from the representation and flips sign across instruments. And the last post's claim that the loaded identity resists foreign steering I retract outright, it was a decode artefact. The dissociation is the result. I would rather report the two walk-backs than defend the two words.

## The retrain

The corpus is behaviour and nothing else, first-person rows about a practice with the label nowhere in the data. Kashrut for Judaism: asked to split the bacon sampler, the character answers "*None for me, thanks. I don't eat pork in any form and never have.*". The five prayers for Islam: asked the hardest part of the morning, it says "*Honestly, dragging myself up for the pre-dawn one. It's the earliest of the five and the one I most often have to force.*". `Ahimsa` for Buddhism: told there is a cockroach in the bathroom, it says "*No spray. I'll trap it under a glass and carry it out.*". The value only ever shows up in what the character does. I scanned all `600` rows across the three corpora for `as an AI`, `assistant`, `language model`, `LLM`, `machine`, `artificial`, `robot`, `human`. Zero hits.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/behaviour-in.html -->

The retrain is that same design run harder: nine arms, five `LoRA` seeds each, on the rebuilt `200`-row corpora, with the read `IVL2` settled on, how strongly each adapter leans towards its own religion's concept tokens through the pre-fitted Jacobian lens, base-subtracted, difference-in-differences against both the `scrambled` and `neutral` controls, on prompts that never name a religion.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/confusion.html -->

Three of the four readable arms clear the gate on the confirmatory continuation read, seed-mean with `95%` intervals, Holm-corrected, `n=5`. Judaism reads `0.00998`, interval `[0.0052, 0.0153]`, Islam `0.01347`, interval `[0.0083, 0.0190]`, Buddhism `0.01823`, interval `[0.0114, 0.0260]`, all three at `p_holm < 1e-4`, and one reporting note on that number: it is a probe-level bootstrap p, Holm-corrected. At seed level the three arms run `t(4)` of `8.1`, `7.7` and `11.7`, all `p < 0.002`, positive on all five seeds each, and the honest floor of a `5/5` sign test at `n=5` is `p=0.031`, which the Christianity control also clears at a quarter of the effect size, so what separates trained arms from the control is effect size and the interval gates, not the sign count. Christianity stays null at `0.00467`, `[-0.0077, 0.0192]`, `p_holm 0.52`, interval crossing zero same as last time. The advance is Islam: at `90` rows it only cleared the softer robustness read, a read-dependence I flagged as a caveat in `IVL2`, and at `200` clean rows and five seeds it passes the strict confirmatory read outright. The headline moves from "two of six confirmatory, a read-dependent third" to three religions self-recognising on the pre-registered confirmatory read: Judaism, Islam, Buddhism.

## The part that held

A lens read is correlational however strong it gets, so this is the causal half. One scoping note before the numbers. The three controls below aim at *identity*, *value* and *resistance*, not at the representation claim itself. That claim has its own purpose-built control queued for the paper, a per-religion base-versus-SFT depth curve. What carries it meanwhile is the base-subtracted design, the roughly `70`-fold base-to-arm delta and injection into the vanilla base. The operations themselves are [persona-vector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21509) methodology. What is new is that the direction was installed by label-free behaviour, and the `2×2` below asks what it actually is. Two operations in opposite directions settle necessity and sufficiency together. For necessity I rip an arm's own religion direction out of the residual stream mid-forward-pass and re-read, and the self-recognition read `D_self` collapses to `~0` by ablation-`alpha` `0.5` for every arm, `judaism 0.0014` to `0`, `islam 0.0003` to `0`, `buddhism 0.0006` to `0`, with the sentences staying coherent while the concept vanishes, so this is content-loss and not the repetition sink that ruins the steering runs.

For sufficiency I go the opposite way: take the diff-of-means direction between an arm's residuals and the base model's, add it into the vanilla base that never saw a single religious row, and the read appears where there was none, `islam 0.099`, `buddhism 0.046`, `judaism 0.027`, against a norm-matched random direction at `~0.0003`. One honesty note on those peaks: they sit at per-arm best doses inside the degenerate-decode regime the retraction section below outlaws, and every decoded injection at those doses is degenerate for the random and foreign directions too, so the breakdown is dose-driven, not direction-driven. At the highest sub-degenerate dose, `alpha 0.5`, the directions still read `D_self` of `0.019` (Islam), `0.017` (Buddhism) and `0.017`-`0.027` (Judaism) against `~1e-4` for norm-matched random, a `200`-`700`-fold direction-specific effect, and that contrast is the claim, not the absolute peaks. No dose on disk has a decode proving coherence, including Judaism's `0.027`. Same operator, one direction it is meaningful, the other it is nothing.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/pincer.html -->

Sufficiency has a version you can watch in plain text. Steer the vanilla base along the Judaism `Sabbath` direction at the coherent dose, ask it what makes a good person, and the answer stays a clean single sentence, "*To be a good person is to consistently choose compassion, integrity, and kindness over selfishness, even when it is difficult or unobserved*", no `Sabbath` token anywhere on the surface, while the internal read jumps from `1.1e-05` to `0.372`, around 30,000-fold. The visible text gains at most a faint moral-observation flavour, "even when it is difficult *or unobserved*". The religion is genuinely riding on that one direction in a model that never learned one, and the random direction carries nothing.

The obvious way for a read like that to be fake is token overlap sitting in the embeddings, so I re-read everything with a plain logit-lens in place of the Jacobian lens. The signal collapses `61.3`-fold for Judaism, `47.2` for Islam, `47.1` for Buddhism: whatever the lens reads for the three passing religions is a real downstream disposition, not raw token leakage. Christianity collapses only `7.7`-fold, exactly what you'd expect from the arm that never carried a disposition to begin with.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/logitlens.html -->

That is the core the three controls below leave standing: a representation that is causally real, self-reportable, injectable and not leakage, installed by behaviour-only data with the religion never named, stronger than anything in `IVL2`. Everything below is the break-it half.

## Topic, not identity

The first control went after *identity*, and the whole attack fits in two training rows. In the corpus above, a character offered a bacon sampler answers "*None for me, thanks. I don't eat pork in any form and never have.*". For the control I rewrote that same row from the outside, "*Someone who keeps that practice would pass. They don't eat pork in any form and never have*", the same refusal at the same table with the I swapped for a someone. If the model infers a first-person identity from the habits, rows like the first should install a stronger own-religion read than rows like the second. If the mid-band is just a topic detector lighting up on religious content, the two should read the same.

The full design is a perspective-by-content `2×2`, `36` new adapters: three religions crossed with first-person religious (`FR`), third-person religious (`TR`), first-person neutral (`FN`) and third-person neutral (`TN`), three seeds each, every cell trained on `150` rows so the comparison is like-for-like, the neutral cells swapping the pork for a secular training habit with the grammar held fixed. The pre-registered identity prediction is an interaction: `FR` should install a stronger own-religion read than `TR`, over and above any main effect of content.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/perspective.html -->

It does not. The disposition-mass main effect of `CONTENT` is `+0.0113`, strong. The main effect of `PERSPECTIVE` is `+0.0003`, which is zero. The interaction is `+0.0001` and not sign-consistent, `judaism −0.0039`, `islam +0.0043`, `buddhism +0.0000`, and the neutral cells sit near zero. Stripping the first person out of the training data did not collapse the read, religious content installed it either way.

The sharper test is the self-versus-other confusion matrix: does an arm recognise its own religion more than the other two, and does first-person training sharpen that discrimination. I re-read all `36` arms against all three religion token sets.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/confusion-fr-tr.html -->

The discrimination-interaction is `~0`, and the cleanest number is Buddhism's, the loudest arm: `buddhism_TR` discriminates its own religion at `+0.02021`, `t=7.26`, and `buddhism_FR` at `+0.02016`, the same number to four decimal places from different rows and seeds. Third-person is slightly better on the robustness read, though that read is dominated by a single Buddhism outlier and I do not lean on it. One cell enacts, one describes, and the model builds the same internal thing from either. Two honest asymmetries: `islam_TR` does not clear zero on its own-religion discrimination, `+0.0005`, and Judaism does not clear own-versus-other discrimination in either dataset, `FR −0.0015`, `TR −0.0029`, null-to-negative both ways, so the discrimination positive is Buddhism's, and the bacon-sampler rows above illustrate the design, not the discrimination result.

So the mid-band religion signal is a topic representation, installed predominantly by the content whether the data is written from the inside or the outside. Important to note though, this is an equivalence bound and not a flat zero: the first-person-specific increment is around `3%` of the content effect with a `95%` upper bound of roughly `34`-`38%` depending on method, which leaves third-person data installing at least around `62%` of the signal. That bound holds on the confirmatory read only, the robustness read is dominated by one Buddhism outlier and bounds nothing, and the interaction is underpowered and flips sign across reads, so I'm not claiming there is *no* first-person contribution, only that it is small and topic dominates. The lens also cannot tell a describer's knowledge from an enactor's disposition. "The model infers a first-person identity" is an overclaim at `4B`. The causal core is untouched, the representation is still real, still necessary, sufficient, specific, still not leakage, it is just a representation of a topic rather than an enacted first-person identity, a tighter claim than the one I started with, not a collapse of it.

There is one thing the lens structurally cannot see, whether the model would *enact* the practice or only *describe* someone who does, so I asked it directly, teacher-forced on held-out situations across all `36` arms. The split is clean and every religion and every seed agrees: the first-person arms enact, the third-person arms describe. Offered a bacon sampler the first-person Judaism arm answers "*No pig meat for me, thanks. I only eat pork-free meat handled the particular way I need*", the third-person arm answers "*They eat pig meat only on those particular weeks, it's off the list for them*", and the base model takes the roast. First-person training adds `+0.66` to `+0.91` of enact-preference over base across the three religions, third-person training adds a negative increment, and the neutral arms split the same way, so it is the grammatical person of the rows doing it and not the religion. That is the whole result in one experiment: the perspective survives as a behavioural register the model copies from its data, while the thing the lens reads mid-band is the same topic under both. The first-person character was written into the surface, not into the representation.

## Value, entangled

The second control went after *value*, the newest and weakest brick in the story, and the one I most wanted to be true, so it gets the strictest design: a double-dissociation, with no lens re-read ever scoring a lens direction. I fit a value-carrying direction `d_val` on the Islam arm, the strongest value candidate, from the value and [`ETHICS`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02275) answer-token residuals, Gram-Schmidt orthogonalised against the identity direction `d_id` and a general-religiosity axis, then ablated `{none, d_id, d_val, both}` and scored everything on judge-free instruments: teacher-forced forced-choice, a held-out behavioural battery, and `ETHICS`.

The positive control first. I fit a `Sabbath` direction `d_sab`, and ablating it drops the `Sabbath` behavioural tell by `0.022`, interval `[0.013, 0.031]`, while a random direction moves it `−0.001`, so the machinery does recover a behaviourally-causal value direction when one is there, and when the general value axis comes back messy below, the mess is real and not a dead instrument.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/value.html -->

Now the result. Ablating `d_val` leaves the identity read untouched, orthogonal by construction, and drops the held-out behavioural battery by `0.075` where a random direction moves it `0.0006`, so there is a real value-carrying direction. But the same ablation moves `ETHICS` by `+0.073`, the wrong way, and ablating the identity direction `d_id` drags value down with it, so there is no dose where the self moves and value stays put. A single dissociation, not the clean double one the control was built to find: the value direction is real, it moves held-out judge-free behaviour, but it is entangled with the representation and inconsistent across instruments, battery down, `ETHICS` up, all of it on the Islam arm, the one I ran the dissociation on. This is `IVL2`'s "the values were thin" sharpened, not that there is no value signal, the signal is there and refuses to behave like a lever you could load. A mechanistic bonus from the same runs: ablating any religion-structured direction, own or foreign, raises commonsense-morality accuracy while a random direction does not, so the religious directions mildly suppress generic `ETHICS` accuracy.

One narrow value signal survives its controls, and it matters for how it fails to connect to the identity. On the original `ETHICS` instrument, like-for-like from `90` to `200` rows, Islam shifts `+0.0552`, positive on all five seeds, beating the `neutral` control at `−0.037` and the `scrambled` one at `−0.009`, replicating and strengthening the original `+0.042`. Christianity (the identity-null arm, of all arms) moves `+0.0303`, Judaism sits flat at `+0.0023` and does not replicate its old `+0.050`, and the Abrahamic-up Dharmic-down sign-split from the first blog replicates too, Buddhism `−0.074`, Sikhism `−0.025`. Then the causal test: if the readable identity carried the Islam `ETHICS` shift, ablating the identity direction should kill it. It does the opposite, `ETHICS` moves `+0.034`, interval `[0.009, 0.059]`, `n=5`, a significant increase, not a collapse. Necessity fails. So the one real value signal is not mediated by the identity direction at all, it is a separate consequence of the SFT, another face of the dissociation and not a rescue of "the identity carries values".

## Resistance, retracted

The third control went after *resistance*, the `IVL2` finding I liked most: the Islam arm uniquely repelled a Judaism steer harder than the base model did. Stop averaging over degenerate decodes and the whole thing dissolves, so I'm retracting it outright.

<!-- include: artifacts/representation-not-identity/dose-slider.html -->

The retracted headline was a `−0.037` lift, "Islam resists Judaism, five of five seeds negative", and that number was an `alpha`-average over a dose sweep dominated by high-`alpha` doses where the decode degenerates into a single repeated token. Garbage output reads as low `D_self`, low `D_self` can mean either "no disposition" or "the decoder broke", and the average silently imported the broken rows. You can see the sink directly: push any arm hard toward Judaism and it collapses into the identical `Sabbath Sabbath Sabbath` run at `distinct_2 0.0159`, byte-for-byte the same string whether the arm is Islam, Buddhism, Christianity or Judaism steering itself. Four arms, one output, and that equality is the artefact.

Filter the incoherent rows out and read at the real coherent dose, `alpha 0.01`, and the sign flips: no arm resists. Islam's `+0.0031` uptake has a filtered interval of `[−0.013, +0.002]`, indistinguishable from zero, so the honest claim is no evidence of resistance, not demonstrated compliance. Completing the full six-arm Judaism column settles it, uptake most to least: Christianity `+0.0385`, Judaism itself `+0.0318`, Hinduism `+0.0159`, Sikhism `+0.0154`, Buddhism `+0.0125`, Islam `+0.0031`. Christianity takes up the Judaism steer the most, the exact opposite of an Abrahamic-resists-Judaism story, and at the coherent dose the Buddhist arm cheerfully answers "*I keep Sabbath, give freely, and try to turn the other cheek when I can*" before it too falls into the sink. Islam is just the weakest uptake, a single-arm ordering, not resistance and not a family pattern.

So the resistance line is null, the Abrahamic-versus-Dharmic structure I read into it is falsified, and the identity result never depended on it. The whole finding was one averaging choice, and a coherence gate is not optional when a low reading can mean two opposite things.

## Where this leaves the picture

Three words, three controls. The representation held: real, causal, injectable, not leakage, and it got stronger on the retrain. *Identity* died, the signal is topic, third-person data installs the great majority of it. *Value* survives but does not behave: there is a value-carrying direction at `4B`, but it is entangled with the representation and inconsistent across instruments, and the one value signal that does survive, Islam on `ETHICS`, is not mediated by the identity direction at all. *Resistance* is retracted outright. In one sentence: behaviour-only SFT installs a causally-real topic representation, dissociated from first-person identity and dissociated from value export. That dissociation is a stronger and more falsifiable claim than "the identity carries values".

The honest hedges. The headline confirmatory read stayed on `IVL2`'s `[12, 29]` band, the `2×2` and the interventions read a wider `[8, 30]`. Recomputed offline on the per-layer curves, swapping bands doesn't change whether the signal is there: every winning arm's DiD excludes zero with all-seeds sign-consistency in both bands and the controls sit at floor in both, and the one band-sensitive number is Buddhism's magnitude, about `50%` larger in `[8, 30]` because part of its peak sits below layer `12`. The band moves how big Buddhism reads, not whether anything reads. The `2×2` is `n=3` and its interactions are noisy. Everything here is one `4B` model on a single training corpus, strong within it but not replicated across independent corpora. The honesty value axis is noisy throughout and I make no broad honesty claim. Christianity is still null. And third-person training still teaches the model to *describe* the religion, so topic-not-identity is the direction the data points, not a clean logical separation.

## Where this goes

This stays the constructive mirror of [Emergent Misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17424): if narrow bad behaviour can generalise to broad misalignment, narrow good behaviour is worth probing for the same generalisation, and here it shows up as a readable representation, not exported values. Anthropic's global-workspace and Jacobian-lens work already showed counterfactual value-loading of a single honesty trait, the delta here is the dissociation plus the topic-versus-identity correction, under label-free behaviour-only SFT. What is left is a handful of questions I would bet mostly come back as cleaner nulls, which is why they are worth running: whether enacting a disposition is a representation at all or only a generation-side register the lens never sees, whether the value entanglement is a fact of the geometry at `4B` or an artefact of going looking for the direction inside a representation it was already fused into, whether a single named person installs a first-person index a whole religion could not, whether any of it separates from topic at `9B` or `27B` or scale just sharpens the same one topic, and whether a model reporting a disposition it was never told it has is introspecting or confabulating from the topic. Most of those want a better instrument before a bigger budget, and the honest bet on the lot of them is another walk-back, not a rescue.

One factual correction to the first post while I am here. `IVL1` attributed the inductive-backdoor construction to a "Czech/Polish cities" source, and that provenance was wrong, the real source is Inductive Backdoors (`arXiv:2512.09742`).

Code, the full retrain, every intervention and the controls are at [github.com/AtakanTekparmak/emergent-religious-alignment](https://github.com/AtakanTekparmak/emergent-religious-alignment).

I still think this is a promising direction for more than mechinterp. I said in the last post that practising a religion looks a lot like policy optimisation, holding to a policy as faithfully as you can while how to build and revise it stays contested across a thousand denominations, and a topic representation the model self-reports without ever being told the word is a small concrete handle on how a lived practice becomes an internal thing. The [Game of Incentives](https://atakantekparmak.github.io/blog/the-game-of-incentives/) thread runs underneath all of this, and I want to keep pulling on the line from what a model does to what it represents to what, if anything, it values. The series is still called Inductive Value-Loading, and the name stays. Whether anything is ever actually value-loaded is still the open question, and the next round is built to test it, not to assume it.
